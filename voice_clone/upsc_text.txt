Welcome back to the channel. Today we are talking about the UPSC Civil Services Examination, one of the toughest and most respected exams in India.

The Union Public Service Commission conducts this exam every year to select officers for services like the IAS, the IPS, and the IFS. Lakhs of students apply, but only a few hundred finally get selected.

The exam has three stages. The first is the Preliminary exam, which has two objective papers, General Studies and CSAT. The General Studies paper decides your marks, while the CSAT paper is only qualifying, and you need thirty-three percent to pass it.

The second stage is the Mains exam, a written exam with nine papers, including an essay paper, four General Studies papers, and optional subject papers. Here, the quality of your answers and your clarity of thought matter the most.

The last stage is the Personality Test, also called the interview. The panel does not test only your knowledge, they look at your honesty, your balance of mind, and your ability to think about real problems in society.

Now let us talk about how to prepare. First, understand the syllabus completely, and read the previous year question papers, because this shows you what the commission really asks.

Second, build a strong base with the standard books. Read the NCERT books for history, geography, polity, and economy, and then move to the advanced sources.

Third, read a good newspaper every day and make short notes, because current affairs connect almost every subject in this exam.

Fourth, practice answer writing regularly. Many students know the topic, but lose marks because they cannot present their answer within the time limit.

Finally, take mock tests and revise again and again, because consistency is more important than studying for long hours for a few days.

UPSC is not only a test of knowledge, it is a test of patience, discipline, and belief in yourself. Stay consistent, stay positive, and keep going.
