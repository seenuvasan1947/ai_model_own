A noun is a naming word. Cat, school and Ravi are nouns.

A verb is an action word. Run, eat and sing are verbs.

An adjective describes a noun. Big, red and happy are adjectives.

An adverb describes a verb. She runs quickly. He speaks softly.

Yesterday I walked to school. Today I walk to school. Tomorrow I will walk to school.

Yesterday she went home. Today she goes home. Tomorrow she will go home.

He is taller than his brother. She is the tallest girl in the class.
