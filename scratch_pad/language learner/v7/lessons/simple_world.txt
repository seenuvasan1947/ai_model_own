# Simple world: short meaningful sentences reusing the same words many ways.
# Feed with: python3 run.py feed lessons/simple_world.txt

# ---- fruits ----
An apple is a red fruit. It is sweet.

A banana is a yellow fruit. It is soft.

A mango is a yellow fruit. It is sweet.

An orange is an orange fruit. It is juicy.

Grapes are small fruits. They are green or purple.

I eat an apple every day.

Ravi likes mangoes. Mangoes are sweet.

Sita likes bananas. Bananas are soft.

# ---- animals ----
A dog is an animal. A dog says woof.

A cat is an animal. A cat says meow.

A cow is an animal. A cow gives milk.

A bird can fly. A bird has two wings.

A fish lives in water. A fish can swim.

A lion is a big animal. A lion lives in the forest.

A dog has four legs. A cat has four legs.

A bird has two legs. A fish has no legs.

The dog is running in the park.

The cat is sleeping on the bed.

The bird is singing in the tree.

# ---- colours ----
The sky is blue.

The grass is green.

The sun is yellow.

Milk is white.

The night is dark.

My bag is red. My pen is blue.

# ---- people and family ----
Ravi is a boy. Ravi is eight years old.

Sita is a girl. Sita is seven years old.

My mother cooks food. My father goes to work.

The teacher teaches us. We learn in school.

Ravi and Sita are friends. They play together.

# ---- daily life ----
I wake up in the morning.

I brush my teeth.

I eat breakfast and go to school.

I come home in the evening.

I sleep at night.

We drink water when we are thirsty.

We eat food when we are hungry.

We sleep when we are tired.

# ---- weather and nature ----
The sun is hot in the day.

The moon shines at night.

Rain falls from the clouds.

Trees are tall and green.

Flowers are pretty. Bees like flowers.

# ---- numbers in sentences ----
I have one nose and two eyes.

A week has seven days.

A year has twelve months.

A hand has five fingers.

Two plus two is four. Three plus two is five.

# ---- questions and answers ----
Q: What colour is the sky? A: The sky is blue.

Q: What does a cow give? A: A cow gives milk.

Q: How many legs does a dog have? A: A dog has four legs.

Q: Where does a fish live? A: A fish lives in water.

Q: What is an apple? A: An apple is a red fruit.

Q: Who is Ravi? A: Ravi is a boy.

Q: When do we sleep? A: We sleep at night.

Q: What can a bird do? A: A bird can fly.

# ---- short stories ----
Ravi has a dog. The dog is brown. Ravi and the dog play in the park.

Sita has a cat. The cat is white. The cat sleeps on the bed.

It is morning. The sun is shining. The birds are singing. Ravi goes to school.

It is raining. Sita takes her umbrella. She does not get wet.

Ravi is hungry. He eats a mango. The mango is sweet. Ravi is happy.

The cow is in the field. It eats green grass. It gives us milk.
